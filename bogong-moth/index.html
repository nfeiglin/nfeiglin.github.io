WHY THIS MATTERS

A small traveller.
An alpine lifeline.

Bogong moths are a seasonal food source for the endangered mountain pygmy-possum and other alpine animals. Their migrations also connect people to Country: Aboriginal peoples have long gathered and eaten bogong moths in the high country.

The species is threatened by factors including drought, habitat change and artificial lighting. Not all bogong moths migrate; the story above follows the best-studied alpine migratory population.
